Team Management
Organizes users into teams within organizations for collaboration.

Manages team creation, membership, and listing within organizations. Teams group users for collaborative access control and resource sharing, with support for adding and removing members.

This module provides sub-organization grouping of users in Ayunis. Key entities are `Team` (name, org ID, timestamps) and `TeamMember` (linking a user to a team with an optional hydrated `User` reference). Use cases include `CreateTeamUseCase` (provisions a named team within an org), `UpdateTeamUseCase` (rename), `DeleteTeamUseCase`, `GetTeamUseCase`, `ListTeamsUseCase` (all teams in an org), `ListMyTeamsUseCase` (teams for the current user), `AddTeamMemberUseCase`, `RemoveTeamMemberUseCase`, `ListTeamMembersUseCase`, `CheckUserTeamMembershipUseCase` (verifies if a user belongs to a specific team), `FindAllUserIdsByTeamIdUseCase` (returns all user IDs for a team without pagination, used by other modules for batch operations like share-scope resolution), and `FindAllUserIdsByTeamIdsUseCase` (returns current user IDs grouped across requested organization-scoped teams through one set-based query). `FindTeamsByOrgIdUseCase` provides a context-free lookup boundary for trusted cross-module orchestration, and `AssignUserToTeamsUseCase` validates both team and user organization ownership before idempotently inserting multiple memberships in one transaction-aware query. `TeamsApplicationModule` owns the repository adapters and exports only use-case boundaries, including the context-free operations used by **invites** and **SSO**; `TeamsModule` composes it with the HTTP controller and response mappers. The module integrates with **orgs** for organizational scoping, **users** for member identity and hydration, and the **domain** layer where team membership can influence resource visibility. Two repository ports—`TeamsRepositoryPort` and `TeamMembersRepositoryPort`—abstract persistence with PostgreSQL implementations.

`Team.modelOverrideEnabled` selects model-policy scope without snapshotting organization grants. Team language and image-generation grants are explicit records independent of organization grants, and disabling an override preserves them. Image resolution already treats an enabled team with no image grant as unavailable; language resolution still falls back to organization grants when the team allowlist is empty until the effective-resolution checkpoint completes the policy. Embedding models remain organization-wide.

**Usage visibility (AYC-1342).** Hiding individual usage does not restrict team management, membership, team limits, user deletion or invitation team assignments. Normal role and organization ownership checks still apply. Super-admin team management endpoints remain available.

`FindTeamsByIdsAndOrgIdUseCase` exports a context-free, organization-scoped batch lookup for single-invite team validation. Its callers own authorization and reject requested IDs missing from the result.

`FindTeamsByUserIdsAndOrgIdUseCase` exposes a trusted, context-free batch lookup of team id/name summaries grouped by user ID. The teams repository scopes both memberships and users to the requested organization in one query; the calling user-list use case owns administrator-only enrichment.

**Team roles (AYC-1599).** `TeamMember.role` is a `UserRole` (`user` by default; the `AddRoleToTeamMembers` migration backfilled existing members). `TeamAccessService` resolves a `TeamAccess` per team: the org role's permissions plus the team role's (team `admin` ⇒ every permission, team `manager`/`user` ⇒ that row of the org's role matrix via `GetMyPermissionsUseCase`). It takes the principal explicitly because `TeamPermissionsGuard` runs before the context interceptor. `@RequireTeamPermission(...)` + `TeamPermissionsGuard` (bound globally in `IamModule`) gate the `/teams/:id` reads, rename and the member routes in `TeamMembersController`; team creation, deletion and the model-override switch stay organisation-wide (`@RequirePermission` / `UpdateTeamUseCase`). `TeamRolePolicyService` narrows member management held only through a team role: changing roles (`UpdateTeamMemberRoleUseCase`, `PATCH /teams/:id/members/:userId`) and removing team admins need team admin or organisation-wide `assign_users_to_teams`, and such team-scoped actors cannot remove or demote the team's last admin (`TeamLastAdminError`, 409). Concurrent demotions of the last two admins are not serialised; organisation-wide member managers can always repair a team. The super-admin routes pass a target `orgId` and skip the team policy. `GET /teams` (`ListManageableTeamsUseCase`) returns every team to organisation-wide holders of `manage_teams`/`assign_users_to_teams` and otherwise only the teams the caller's team role lets them manage; `GET /teams/me` (`ListMyTeamAccessUseCase`) returns each team with the caller's team role and effective permissions. `GetTeamAccessUseCase` is exported for other modules (shares). `GET /teams/:id/role-permissions` (`GetTeamRolePermissionsUseCase`, gated by team-scoped `assign_users_to_teams`) returns what each team role grants in the team: the `TEAM_SCOPED_PERMISSIONS` part of that role's matrix row (admin: all of them). It lets team admins, who cannot read the admin-only role matrix, see what a role means when assigning it.

**Team deletion (AYC-1642).** `DeleteTeamUseCase` emits `TeamDeletionRequestedEvent` (a `DeferredCleanupEvent`) before the row delete and runs the registered cleanup tasks only after it. Team-owned resources (e.g. team skills) cascade with the team row; listeners in their modules resolve data outside the cascade, such as skill sources, while the rows still exist.

**Team- and org-owned resources (AYC-1642).** `GetCollectiveOwnerAccessUseCase` is the shared policy for resources owned by a team or the organization (skills, knowledge bases): team members read what their team owns (membership is checked together with the team's organization, `TeamMembersRepository.isMemberInOrg`, so it never reaches across tenants) and organization members read what the organization owns; managing takes the given team permission as resolved by `TeamAccessService` for the user's role in that team (team admins hold all) plus membership, or the given org permission. Callers pass the permission pair for their resource. `ListReadableCollectiveOwnersUseCase` returns the caller's organization and their team IDs in it, for list and run-time queries.
