Knowledge Bases
Document collections for organized RAG retrieval

Knowledge bases group uploaded documents (PDF, DOCX, PPTX, CSV, XLSX, XLS, ODS; tables are flattened to per-row "Header: value" text by the document processing consumer (the file retriever is skipped) and searched like any other document) into named collections that can be queried semantically. Each knowledge base belongs to an organization and exactly one owner: a user, a workspace, a team, or the organization itself. Because every row carries its tenant in `orgId`, organization ownership is marked explicitly with `isOrgOwned`; `CHK_knowledge_bases_single_owner` (`num_nonnulls("userId", "workspaceId", "teamId") + "isOrgOwned"::int = 1`) rejects rows with no owner or several.

**Team- and org-owned knowledge bases (AYC-1642).** `TeamKnowledgeBase` and `OrgKnowledgeBase` (both `AbstractCollectiveKnowledgeBase`, union `CollectiveKnowledgeBase`) are read by team members and organization members respectively and managed (edit, delete, activate, link) by members holding `manage_knowledge_bases` plus `share_knowledge_bases_with_teams` (as resolved for their team role, so team admins qualify) or `share_knowledge_bases_with_org`. Read checks resolve only membership or organization, without loading permissions. The policy is the teams module's `GetCollectiveOwnerAccessUseCase`; `CollectiveKnowledgeBaseAccessService` supplies the knowledge-base permissions and maps the result to 404 (not readable) or 403 (readable, not manageable), and the read/write access services delegate to it. Creating for a team or the organization (`ownerType=team` with `teamId`, or `ownerType=org`) checks that right first. Their activation is global and stored on the row (`knowledge_bases.isActive`, only authoritative for these owners); they start active, and switching it applies to everyone with access. They cannot be shared, and the share branches of the access queries only match personal rows, so a share left on a row that changed owner never widens access. Rename and activation lock the row before saving it. Personal lists (`ownerType=personal`) include the team and org knowledge bases the user can read; run-time resolution (`FindActiveKnowledgeBasesUseCase`, `FindAccessibleKnowledgeBasesByIdsUseCase`, citations) includes the active ones, and members can attach them to chats directly. Org ownership is always matched together with the organization ID (`whereCollectiveKnowledgeBaseOwner`), because the personal list query has no tenant filter of its own; `local-knowledge-base-access.integration.spec.ts` proves the access SQL against the migrated schema. When a team is deleted its knowledge bases cascade with it, and `KnowledgeBasesTeamDeletionRequestedListener` defers cleanup of sources still being processed through `KnowledgeBaseProcessingCleanupService`, which the workspace listener shares.

**Owner changes.** `KnowledgeBaseOwnerController` exposes `PATCH /knowledge-bases/:id/owner` (`ChangeKnowledgeBaseOwnerUseCase`) and `GET /knowledge-bases/:id/owner-change-preview` (`PreviewKnowledgeBaseOwnerChangeUseCase`, which only counts the skill links a move would remove). Moving between the caller, a team and the organization needs the right to manage both owners; workspace knowledge bases cannot move (`KNOWLEDGE_BASE_OWNER_CHANGE_NOT_ALLOWED`, only after access is checked). The move locks the row. The knowledge base keeps its id, documents and index; `KnowledgeBaseOwnerChangeService` carries the mover's activation over as global state. Every move clears all per-user activations (a moved knowledge base has no shares left), and moving back to personal sets the mover's activation to exactly the former global state. The skills module's `UnlinkKnowledgeBaseFromOtherOwnersUseCase` removes it from skills of other owners, and `RemoveEntitySharesUseCase` removes its shares and reconciles everyone outside the new owner's audience. `FindKnowledgeBasesByIdsUseCase` is exported for trusted ownership comparisons only (skill owner changes).

`AbstractKnowledgeBase` holds the shared name, optional description, organization scope (`orgId`), and timestamps. `KnowledgeBaseReadAccessService` and `KnowledgeBaseWriteAccessService` dispatch authorization from the persisted owner type: personal resources retain local ownership/sharing rules, while workspace resources delegate to exported workspace read, write, or trusted-execution capabilities. Its concrete `PersonalKnowledgeBase` and `WorkspaceKnowledgeBase` subclasses expose a non-null `userId` or `workspaceId`, respectively; nullable owner columns remain confined to persistence. `FindKnowledgeBaseForThreadUseCase` exposes trusted-thread read access for skill-driven knowledge-base attachment, including knowledge bases in that thread's workspace; direct personal attachment still uses `FindAccessibleKnowledgeBaseUseCase`. Mixed lookups retain the `KnowledgeBase` union, and callers narrow ownership with `instanceof PersonalKnowledgeBase`, `WorkspaceKnowledgeBase`, `TeamKnowledgeBase` or `OrgKnowledgeBase` (or `AbstractCollectiveKnowledgeBase` for either of the last two). The canonical create boundary accepts a `personal`, `workspace`, `team` or `org` owner discriminator; the list boundary a `personal` or `workspace` one. Personal knowledge bases cannot be copied or attached to workspaces; workspace knowledge bases are created directly within their workspace. Activation is stored by row presence in `knowledge_base_activations` for both user and workspace scopes. Personal and shared knowledge-base activation remains per-user, while workspace activation is workspace-wide; ownership remains on `knowledge_bases.userId` or `knowledge_bases.workspaceId`. The activation repository operations support idempotent activation, deactivation, status lookup, and batched active-ID lookup. Run-time active knowledge-base resolution uses one database query with activation, direct org/team share, team-membership, and shared-skill access checks expressed as joins and correlated `EXISTS` clauses built by the TypeORM query helpers in `queries/knowledge-base-accessible.db-query.ts` (which reuses the share subqueries of `queries/knowledge-base-access.db-query.ts`); citation authorization constrains that query to one knowledge-base and source relationship instead of loading the full active set, and indexes on both share foreign-key columns support these lookups. The `KnowledgeBaseSummary` interface (in `domain/knowledge-base-summary.ts`) is a lightweight projection containing only `id` and `name`, used by other modules (e.g., threads) to reference knowledge bases without loading the full entity. The `KnowledgeBaseRepository` port defines mixed and ownership-scoped persistence: save, find by ID/IDs with organization and workspace filters, personal/workspace owner lists, accessible paginated lists, and deletion. Personal and workspace activation have separate activate/deactivate methods, active-ID queries, and batched workspace-state lookup. Source operations include assignment, single and bulk knowledge-base source snapshots, scoped document lookup, and single/bulk document counts. A local TypeORM implementation (`LocalKnowledgeBaseRepository`) backs the port with a mapper and `KnowledgeBaseRecord` schema; the source-related methods use `SourceRecord` and `SourceMapper` from the sources module. Domain errors are defined in `knowledge-bases.errors.ts`: `KnowledgeBaseNotFoundError` (404 when a knowledge base is not found), `DocumentNotInKnowledgeBaseError` (404 when a document does not belong to a knowledge base), and `MissingFileError` (400 when no file is provided).

Activation is handled by `SetKnowledgeBaseActivationUseCase`, which dispatches from the persisted owner: personal/shared activation stays per-user, while workspace activation requires workspace write access plus `MANAGE_KNOWLEDGE_BASES` and updates workspace-wide state. `FindActiveKnowledgeBasesUseCase` returns only active, currently accessible knowledge bases for run assembly. The canonical CRUD use cases derive principal scope from `ContextService`; entity-ID operations load the entity before owner-aware authorization. `FindKnowledgeBaseUseCase` returns persisted owner, activation, sharing, and document-count context. `ListKnowledgeBasesUseCase` returns `{ data, pagination }`; omitted search and pagination return the complete personal or workspace scope with deterministic name ordering, while either scope uses the same paginated path when search or pagination is supplied. All knowledge-base use-case boundaries use `@HandleUnexpectedErrors(UnexpectedKnowledgeBaseError)`: expected application and HTTP errors pass through unchanged, while unexpected failures are logged once and wrapped with a stable public error code and a non-serialized internal cause.

Four exported access use cases support cross-module callers: `FindAccessibleKnowledgeBaseUseCase` verifies organization scope and resolves personal ownership, direct sharing, or shared-skill access; `FindAccessibleKnowledgeBasesByIdsUseCase` deduplicates IDs and returns the accessible subset of requested personal, team and org knowledge bases; `GetAccessibleKnowledgeBaseContextsUseCase` deduplicates requested IDs, batch-loads organization-scoped resources of every owner, authorizes each through `KnowledgeBaseReadAccessService`, and returns `isShared`, `isActive`, and `documentCount` state using per-user personal activation, workspace-wide activation, the global activation of team and org knowledge bases, and batched document counts; and `FindKnowledgeBaseForThreadUseCase` resolves personal, team or org read access, or workspace read access through a current-user-owned thread in the same workspace and organization. Direct personal thread assignment uses the single-resource lookup; skill-driven assignment, knowledge queries, and document text reads use the thread-aware lookup; skill knowledge-base listing uses the context lookup; and runs use the subset lookup to omit stale thread assignments from user-facing AI context.

`KnowledgeBasesUserDeletionRequestedListener` handles `UserDeletionRequestedEvent` (AYC-382) for the user's personal knowledge bases. Workspace knowledge bases belong to the workspace, which outlives a deleted member; a workspace left without members is deleted through the workspace deletion flow. The listener snapshots the sources' ids and retained-original storage keys before the cascade removes the rows, and defers processing cleanup, original release and index deletion until the row delete succeeds. `KnowledgeBasesWorkspaceDeletionRequestedListener` and `KnowledgeBasesTeamDeletionRequestedListener` only handle queued-job and temporary processing-file cleanup after successful workspace or team deletion. Knowledge bases, their sources, and RAG parent/child chunks (including embeddings) are removed entirely by the existing foreign-key cascade chain; the workspace and team listeners do not call source or index deletion use cases. All three listeners snapshot affected sources in one bulk query before the cascade. The user-deletion listener additionally invokes bulk source/index cleanup. Listener lookup failures are logged and never block deletion.

Direct knowledge-base deletion snapshots source IDs and deletes database records in a short transaction. Existing foreign-key cascades remove sources and RAG chunks atomically; only after commit does best-effort queue/processing-storage cleanup run. Cleanup failures cannot fail an already committed deletion, and failed database transactions do not cancel jobs or purge files (AYC-898).

Four source management use cases handle documents and URLs within a knowledge base: `AddDocumentToKnowledgeBaseUseCase` (uploads a file, validates type, and creates a source linked to the knowledge base), `AddUrlToKnowledgeBaseUseCase` (validates the knowledge base and source limit, creates the PROCESSING source through the sources module under the collection’s capacity lock, then enqueues its asynchronous crawl after commit with an optional `maxDepth`), `RemoveDocumentFromKnowledgeBaseUseCase` (deletes a document by ID, verifies ownership), and `ListKnowledgeBaseDocumentsUseCase` (returns all documents/sources for a knowledge base). Each has a corresponding command/query DTO. Knowledge queries, document text reads, and skill-driven thread attachment resolve access through `FindKnowledgeBaseForThreadUseCase`: personal sharing rules stay unchanged; workspace knowledge requires a current-user-owned execution thread in the same workspace and organization. The thread ID comes from tool execution context, never model input. A `QueryKnowledgeBaseUseCase` authorizes every requested knowledge base through `FindKnowledgeBaseForThreadUseCase` (one inaccessible ID rejects the whole query), then performs one semantic search with all queries across the documents of all requested knowledge bases using the RAG indexer's `SearchContentUseCase`, returning at most ten chunks per query and twenty overall, ranked and tagged with the queries that matched. A `GetKnowledgeBaseDocumentTextUseCase` retrieves the full source entity for a specific document within a knowledge base, verifying access before returning it; this supports the knowledge-get-text tool's need to extract raw document text. Document listing and owner-aware knowledge-base detail reads resolve access through `KnowledgeBaseReadAccessService`, which handles personal knowledge bases owned by the caller, directly shared with them, or linked to a shared skill owned by the knowledge base's owner (via the skills module's `CheckKnowledgeBaseSkillShareAccessUseCase` — sharing a skill implicitly delegates read access to the owner's own linked knowledge bases, and never to a knowledge base someone else merely attached, AYC-13). Shared detail responses are marked read-only for the frontend. Other modules use `FindAccessibleKnowledgeBaseUseCase` for personal/shared access checks and `GetAccessibleKnowledgeBaseContextsUseCase` for access-checked resources with sharing and current-user activation state. The latter deduplicates IDs, batch-loads organization-scoped resources and document counts, and resolves personal and workspace activation according to their respective scopes. `CountKnowledgeBaseDocumentsUseCase` exposes bulk document counts. `AddDocumentToKnowledgeBaseUseCase` is the server-side upload operation for knowledge bases of every owner: it resolves the actual resource, authorizes write access, checks source capacity, starts processing, and assigns the source. Direct browser uploads (AYC-1541) use `CreateKnowledgeBaseUploadUseCase`, which authorizes write access and checks capacity before reserving a session through the sources module, and `CompleteKnowledgeBaseUploadUseCase`, which runs the shared `CompleteSourceUploadUseCase` with the knowledge base's capacity check and assigns the created sources inside the completion transaction. Assignment failures trigger cleanup; cleanup failures are logged without masking the assignment error. The internal `KnowledgeBaseWriteAccessService` requires authenticated organization scope, validates personal ownership directly, delegates team and org permission to `CollectiveKnowledgeBaseAccessService`, and delegates workspace permission to the exported `AssertWorkspaceWriteAccessUseCase`. `KnowledgeBaseReadAccessService` applies the equivalent owner-aware read dispatch and uses `AssertWorkspaceExecutionAccessUseCase` for workspace tool access. Workspace denials are translated to knowledge-base not-found errors so ownership details do not leak. Read sharing never grants write access. Removal checks document membership before deleting.

The HTTP presenter layer exposes the knowledge-base resource API through `KnowledgeBasesController` at `/knowledge-bases`, plus `KnowledgeBaseUploadsController` for direct uploads (`POST /:id/uploads` reserves a session, `POST /:id/uploads/:uploadId/complete` returns the document with 202 once its sources are created). Create requests carry flat `ownerType` (`personal`, `workspace`, `team` or `org`) with a conditionally required `workspaceId` or `teamId`, list requests `personal` (which also lists readable team and org knowledge bases) or `workspace`; entity routes use only the knowledge-base ID and derive ownership from persistence. List responses always use `{ data, pagination }`, and knowledge-base responses include `ownerType`, optional `workspaceId`, activation/sharing state, and `documentCount`. Its ten endpoints are the five CRUD endpoints (`POST /`, `GET /`, `GET /:id`, `PATCH /:id`, `DELETE /:id`) plus `PATCH /:id/activation` and four source endpoints (`GET /:id/documents` to list documents, `POST /:id/documents` for multipart file upload of PDF/DOCX/PPTX/ODT/ODP/CSV/XLSX/XLS/ODS/TXT/EML/MSG/audio, `POST /:id/urls` to add a URL source which returns 202 Accepted since the crawl runs asynchronously, and `DELETE /:id/documents/:documentId` to remove a document, returns 204). The upload endpoint uses the shared `common/http/document-upload.ts` helpers for multipart storage, file-type/MIME validation, and temporary-file cleanup. Request DTOs (`CreateKnowledgeBaseDto`, `UpdateKnowledgeBaseDto`, `AddUrlToKnowledgeBaseDto`) handle input validation, `KnowledgeBaseResponseDto`, `KnowledgeBaseListResponseDto`, `KnowledgeBaseDocumentResponseDto`, and `KnowledgeBaseDocumentListResponseDto` define response shapes, and `KnowledgeBaseDtoMapper` maps domain entities to response DTOs.

The `KnowledgeBasesModule` imports `LocalKnowledgeBaseRepositoryModule`, `SourcesModule`, `IndexersModule`, `ContextModule`, `PermissionsModule`, `TeamsApplicationModule` (team/org access policy), `StorageModule`, and (via `forwardRef`) `SharesModule`, `SkillsModule`, `ThreadsModule`, and `WorkspacesModule`; it registers the canonical use cases, owner-aware read/write services, `CollectiveKnowledgeBaseAccessService`, `KnowledgeBaseProcessingCleanupService`, share strategy, listeners, and DTO mapper. Legacy workspace-specific knowledge-base wrappers and access services do not exist. Other modules consume exported canonical use cases; workspace authorization remains behind the exported workspace capability use cases. The module integrates with **sources** for document storage and chunking, **indexers** for semantic indexing and multi-document vector search, **shares** for direct knowledge-base shares, **skills** for the skill-share access path, and **threads** for cleaning up thread assignments when shares are revoked.

Document and spreadsheet assignments, root URL creation, and discovered-PDF batches share a pessimistic lock on the knowledge-base row for their authoritative source-count check and write. Upload preflight stays outside provider work; a losing final assignment cleans up its unassigned source. Repeating an existing assignment at capacity is a no-op.
